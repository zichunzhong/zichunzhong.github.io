About me

Dr. Zichun Zhong is a Professor and Graduate Program Director of Computer Science at Wayne State University (WSU).

Full biography & professional activities

He was an Assistant and Associate Professor at WSU from August 2015 to July 2026. Prior to joining WSU, he was a postdoctoral fellow in Department of Radiation Oncology at UT Southwestern Medical Center at Dallas (UTSW) from August 2014 to August 2015.

He received Ph.D. degree in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas (UTD) in Summer 2014. He received B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology (Software Engineering) and M.S. degree in Computer Science at The University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in 2006 and 2009, respectively. He received the NSF CAREER Award in 2019, NSF CRII Award in 2017, College of Engineering Faculty Research Excellence Award at WSU in 2019, Certificate of Academic Excellence at UTD in 2013, College of Engineering Award for Excellence in Teaching at WSU in 2020, Best Reviewers in Computers & Graphics 2025 and ACM Multimedia Conference 2020, etc.

Dr. Zhong serves as associate or guest editor for journals such as IEEE TVCG, C&G, Graphical Models, CAGD, Neural Processing Letters, and holds leadership roles in conferences, such as a Communication Chair of Shape Modeling International Conference (SMI) 2026, Technical Paper Chair of Shape Modeling International Conference (SMI) 2024, Shape Modeling International Conference (SMI) 2025, Shape Modeling International Conference (SMI) 2027 and a Program Co-Chair of International Conference on Geometric Modeling and Processing (GMP) 2024. He is in the SMI Council since 2024 and the SMI Steering Committee since 2025.
